# AI Photo Prompts: 35 Copy-Paste Ideas for Realistic Images

An AI photo prompt is more than a short description of what you want in an image. The best photo prompts give the model a clear subject, camera direction, lighting, mood, location, and visual finish. That extra structure is what turns a flat image request into something that feels photographed, composed, and intentional.

This guide gives you copy-ready AI photo prompts you can adapt for portraits, product photography, lifestyle content, travel scenes, fashion editorials, and social media visuals. Use them as starting points, then swap the subject, setting, lens, color palette, and aspect ratio to match your project.

If you want to browse more examples, start with the full [AI image prompts library](/explore) and filter by your preferred style or category.

## How to Write a Better AI Photo Prompt

A strong photo prompt usually has six parts:

- Subject: who or what appears in the image.
- Setting: where the subject is placed.
- Lighting: the mood and direction of light.
- Camera language: lens, angle, framing, or depth of field.
- Style: editorial, documentary, cinematic, commercial, candid, or studio.
- Constraints: what to avoid, such as distorted hands, extra limbs, bad text, or blurry faces.

Here is a simple structure:

> A realistic photo of [subject] in [setting], [lighting], [camera angle/lens], [mood/style], high detail, natural colors, clean composition.

You do not need to stuff every keyword into the prompt. Clear creative direction works better than a pile of adjectives.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

Do not rely on vague prompts like "make a cool photo." The model needs direction. Avoid mixing too many styles in one request, because "cinematic, watercolor, product photo, anime, hyperrealistic, sketch" gives conflicting signals.

It also helps to avoid overusing quality words. Terms like "8k," "masterpiece," and "ultra detailed" can be useful, but they cannot replace a clear subject, composition, and lighting plan.
