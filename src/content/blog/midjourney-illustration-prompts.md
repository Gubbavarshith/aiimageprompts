# Midjourney Illustration Prompts for Editorial and Brand Visuals

Midjourney illustration prompts work best when they sound like art direction. A strong prompt tells the model the subject, medium, composition, palette, texture, and mood.

This guide focuses on illustration prompts for editorial content, brand campaigns, posters, and visual storytelling. For broader Midjourney templates, read [Best Midjourney Prompts](/blog/best-midjourney-prompts). For prompt browsing, use [Explore](/explore).

## The Illustration Prompt Formula

```text
[Subject] in [scene], illustrated in [medium/style], [composition], [palette], [texture], [mood], avoid [problems].
```

Example:

> Editorial illustration of a design team planning a launch in a small studio, textured flat-vector style, balanced foreground and background, terracotta, cream, and slate palette, optimistic focused mood, clean silhouettes, no random text, no distorted hands.

## Choose a Medium First

Medium gives the model visual boundaries. Use terms like:

- Ink and watercolor
- Gouache illustration
- Flat vector
- Screen-print poster
- Editorial collage
- Retro travel poster
- Pencil and wash
- Manga linework

Avoid stacking too many mediums. One clear medium is stronger.

## Control Composition

Illustration can become busy fast. Add composition rules:

- Centered hero subject
- Wide establishing scene
- Strong negative space
- Poster-style symmetry
- Isometric view
- Foreground, midground, background layers

Example:

> Retro travel poster illustration of a futuristic coastal city, centered skyline composition, bold geometric shapes, navy, cream, coral, and mustard palette, subtle print grain, large clean sky area for headline, no photoreal textures.

## Brand and Editorial Templates

### Editorial Explainer

```text
Editorial illustration for [topic], featuring [visual metaphor], flat vector with subtle paper grain, clear focal hierarchy, limited palette of [colors], clean negative space, no random text.
```

### Brand Campaign Poster

```text
Poster illustration for [brand idea], [subject], [composition], [medium], [palette], premium but human mood, strong silhouette, copy-safe space, no logos unless specified.
```

### Storybook Scene

```text
Storybook illustration of [scene], watercolor and ink, soft atmospheric perspective, warm gentle palette, hand-painted texture, whimsical but readable composition, no harsh contrast.
```

### Concept Character

```text
Full-body illustration of [character], [costume language], [pose], [world context], clean silhouette, detailed materials, [style/medium], no extra limbs, no muddy background.
```

## GEO-Friendly Local Illustration Prompts

For regional or place-based content, add specific environment cues:

- Architecture: old-town facades, desert modern, coastal concrete, mountain village.
- Materials: red stone, bamboo, linen, stucco, brass, weathered wood.
- Climate: monsoon overcast, dry noon haze, misty morning, blue-hour rain.
- Culture-safe details: markets, textiles, signage shapes, food stalls, local light behavior.

Example:

> Illustrated street food night market in coastal South Asia, rain-reflective pavement, warm tungsten stall lights, hand-painted signage shapes, dense but readable composition, expressive brush texture, cinematic depth, no real brand logos.

## Common Mistakes

Do not lead with vague words like "beautiful." Lead with subject and medium.

Do not mix incompatible styles unless you want experimentation.

Do not forget negative space if the image will become a poster or blog header.

Do not use copyrighted characters for commercial brand work.

## FAQ

### Should I mention Midjourney parameters?

Use them when helpful, especially aspect ratio. The core art direction matters more than parameters.

### What is the best illustration style?

The best style depends on the job: editorial clarity, brand emotion, poster impact, or story mood.

### How do I make a set feel consistent?

Keep medium, palette, texture, and composition rules consistent across prompts.

### Can these prompts work outside Midjourney?

Yes. Remove Midjourney-specific syntax and keep the structure.

## Final Takeaway

Midjourney illustration prompts improve when you define medium, composition, palette, texture, and usage context. Give the model art direction, not only keywords.
