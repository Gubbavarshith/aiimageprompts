# Best Midjourney Prompts for Portraits, Products, and Cinematic Shots

The best Midjourney prompts are structured like art direction. They give the model a subject, style, composition, light, mood, and constraints instead of relying on random adjectives.

Use this guide as a template bank. For broader prompt discovery, visit [Explore](/explore). For realistic image ideas, also read [AI Photo Prompts](/blog/ai-photo-prompts).

## How to Build a Strong Midjourney Prompt

A reliable Midjourney prompt usually includes:

- Subject: the central person, product, place, or idea.
- Context: where the image happens.
- Style: cinematic photo, editorial illustration, poster, concept art.
- Composition: close-up, wide shot, overhead, centered, rule of thirds.
- Lighting: golden hour, neon, soft studio, hard flash, rim light.
- Finish: film grain, premium commercial polish, painterly texture.

## How to Improve These Prompts

Change one variable at a time:

- Swap the subject.
- Keep the same lighting.
- Test a new aspect ratio.
- Adjust style intensity.
- Add or remove one constraint.

This prevents your best prompt from drifting into a completely different direction.

## FAQ

### What makes a Midjourney prompt good?

Clear subject, strong style direction, intentional composition, and controlled lighting.

### Should prompts be long?

Moderate prompts usually work best. Dense and clear beats long and repetitive.

### Can I use these outside Midjourney?

Yes. The structure works across many image tools, though parameters may need changes.
