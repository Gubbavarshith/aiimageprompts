# Text to Image Prompt Formulas With 50 Ready Examples

Prompt formulas help you stop writing from a blank page. They give you a structure that can be reused across portraits, product shots, social visuals, and cinematic scenes.

Use these formulas when you need reliable text-to-image results fast. For broader examples, see [Explore](/explore) and [AI Photo Prompts](/blog/ai-photo-prompts).

## The Master Formula

```text
[Subject] + [Action or state] + [Scene] + [Style] + [Composition] + [Lighting] + [Color] + [Detail] + [Constraints]
```

Example:

> A young architect reviewing blueprints in a concrete studio, editorial documentary photo style, medium shot from desk height, soft overcast window light, cool gray and warm oak palette, realistic paper texture and thoughtful expression, no random text, no distorted hands, no watermark.

## Formula 1: Realistic Portrait

```text
Realistic portrait of [person], [emotion], in [location], [camera framing], [lens feel], [lighting], [color mood], natural skin texture, avoid [artifacts].
```

Ready examples:

1. Realistic portrait of a florist in a small shop, gentle smile, 50mm lens feel, soft morning window light, pastel floral background, no extra fingers.
2. Editorial portrait of a founder in a minimal office, confident expression, 85mm lens feel, warm side light, clean concrete background.
3. Street portrait of a musician in rainy neon city, candid motion, shallow depth of field, cool magenta highlights.
4. Black-and-white portrait of a craftsman in a workshop, hard side light, textured hands, dramatic contrast.
5. Outdoor portrait of a runner at sunrise, natural expression, warm rim light, soft background blur.

## Formula 2: Product Hero

```text
[Product] on [surface], [commercial style], [composition], [lighting], [material detail], [brand mood], avoid [issues].
```

Ready examples:

1. Matte headphones on graphite surface, premium tech photography, centered 3/4 view, blue rim light, crisp material detail.
2. Skincare serum on travertine pedestal, wellness editorial style, soft daylight, realistic glass reflections.
3. Coffee bag beside ceramic cup, cozy lifestyle product photo, morning window light, warm neutral palette.
4. Running shoe frozen mid-splash, energetic sports campaign, high shutter feel, bold contrast.
5. Minimal watch macro on dark velvet, luxury commercial style, sharp metal texture, controlled highlights.

## Formula 3: Cinematic Scene

```text
[Subject/action] in [environment], [time/weather], [shot type], [lighting], [palette], [story details], avoid [problems].
```

Ready examples:

1. Lone explorer beside a vintage truck in desert dust, golden hour, wide cinematic shot, warm film grade.
2. Detective walking through a rainy alley, blue neon light, low-angle frame, noir atmosphere.
3. Astronaut crossing a red ice plain, distant planet rise, epic wide composition, cold cyan palette.
4. Child reading under blanket fort, warm lamp glow, intimate close framing, cozy story mood.
5. Futuristic train station at night, reflective floor, crowded depth, high-tech cinematic realism.

## Formula 4: Social and Ad Visual

```text
Visual for [campaign/topic], featuring [main object], [composition], [contrast], [brand colors], [copy-safe space], avoid [issues].
```

Ready examples:

1. Visual for productivity app launch, glowing notebook and phone, dark navy background, yellow accent, space for headline.
2. Visual for summer skincare sale, serum bottle and citrus, bright natural light, clean top area for copy.
3. Visual for fitness challenge, runner silhouette at sunrise, dynamic diagonal composition, energetic orange palette.
4. Visual for food delivery offer, burger hero shot, moody restaurant lighting, empty right side for text.
5. Visual for creative newsletter, abstract paper shapes, soft shadows, editorial minimal palette.

## Formula 5: Illustration and Concept Art

```text
[Subject/world], [medium/style], [composition], [palette], [texture], [mood], avoid [conflicts].
```

Ready examples:

1. Floating library over mist valley, fantasy matte painting, wide scale, gold and blue palette.
2. Coastal city of the future, retro travel poster style, geometric shapes, coral and navy palette.
3. Forest shrine with fireflies, watercolor and ink, layered composition, magical quiet mood.
4. Cyberpunk street market, detailed concept art, crowded depth, rain reflections, neon palette.
5. Minimal editorial illustration of remote work, flat vector texture, warm optimistic mood.

## How to Create 50 Variations Quickly

Take any five examples and vary:

- Subject
- Location
- Lighting
- Palette
- Composition
- Constraint

Five base formulas multiplied by ten controlled variations gives you 50 usable prompt directions without starting over.

## FAQ

### Are prompt formulas better than creative writing?

They are different. Formulas create control. Creative writing adds taste and story.

### Can one formula work for every model?

The structure transfers well, but exact syntax may need adjustment.

### Should I save formulas or finished prompts?

Save both. Formulas help you create new work, while finished prompts help you repeat proven results.
